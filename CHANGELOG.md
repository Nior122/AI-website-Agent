## [Unreleased]

### Added — 2026-10-15 (Phase 46 continued: relationship stage, send outcomes, one eligibility guard, ledger reads)

- The ledger gained the parts that make it usable as the record of a *relationship* rather than a pile of events:
  a sixth collection `relationships` (one row per lead: stage, previous stage, when it changed, the ids that
  prove it, and the derived next action), portfolio queries (`pipeline`, `outstanding`, `outcome_report`) that are
  read-only, clamped to 200 rows, and paginated, and five new `python -m tracking` commands
  (`relationship`, `relationship-advance`, `pipeline`, `outstanding`, `outreach-eligibility`), taking the ledger
  surface to seventeen commands. The stage row is a projection: it advances when a record proves a step, an
  explicit human move is kept over later derivations, a stage with no record behind it is refused with
  `STAGE_NEEDS_CORROBORATION`, and `pipeline` reports `stage_is_current: false` when the two accounts disagree
  instead of quietly overwriting either.
- Send reporting now distinguishes five states instead of collapsing them. `outreach-mark-sent --result FAILED`
  records an attempt that did not reach anyone (`SEND_FAILED`, `delivery_status: FAILED_REPORTED`,
  `counts_as_contact: false`), leaves the lead uncontacted and the stage unmoved, keeps the draft's human
  approval so a deliberate retry reuses the same outreach record rather than minting a second one, and still
  consumes a contact-budget slot because a retry loop that never trips the cap is how a prospect gets spammed.
  `ACCEPTED_BY_PROVIDER` and `VERIFIED_DELIVERED` are refused with `PROVIDER_RECEIPT_UNAVAILABLE` from every
  entry point - this repository reads no provider receipt, so it does not pretend to know one.
- One guard, no weaker duplicate: `outreach_eligibility()` reads the opt-out, the per-lead per-channel budget, the
  outreach review decision, and the project's state at call time, and both `mark_outreach_sent` and
  `schedule_follow_up` route through it (as must any future sender). It answers and never authorizes: the payload
  carries `grants_authorization: false` and the check list names what a human has to decide.
- Proposals cite their evidence rather than restating it: `--deployment-id` attaches the demo by reading the URL
  and verification state from the recorded deployment (a typed URL that matches no record is
  `DEMO_URL_UNRECORDED`, a mismatched one is `DEMO_URL_MISMATCH`, and `live_url_checked` is echoed, never
  upgraded), `--approval-ref` cites an actual review record for the same project, an `ACCEPTED` proposal needs a
  customer-side evidence kind (`EVIDENCE_KIND_INSUFFICIENT` for an operator's impression), and the draft's
  sending state stays on the outreach record.
- Inbound messages can now carry what a real intake would have: `--provider`, `--external-message-id`,
  `--conversation-id`, `--in-reply-to` (validated to point at something outbound), and `--classification` from a
  fixed vocabulary with `classification_source: operator_asserted` because no classifier exists here. A reply is
  owed until something *outbound* follows it - an internal note does not close it - and an `UNSUBSCRIBE` is not an
  opt-out: it is recorded as an inbound message with `suggested_action: record_opt_out`.
- Durability and safety details: every public write is now one transaction (the touched collections are
  snapshotted, a single durable save lands at the end, and a refusal rolls the in-memory edits back so nothing
  half-applied survives); free-text failure and provider details are refused with `DETAIL_MAY_CONTAIN_SECRET`
  when they look credential-shaped rather than silently scrubbed; contact email/phone redaction still applies to
  all narrative fields; and `TrackingStore.refresh()` is skipped while edits are pending so a re-read cannot
  blend old rows into an unfinished operation.
- Four read-only HTTP routes (`GET /engagement/pipeline`, `/outstanding`, `/eligibility/:lead_id`,
  `/leads/:lead_id`, `/relationship/:lead_id`) were added so a scheduler can ask what is due and what is
  permitted; they sit behind the existing bearer token, ledger refusals answer `400` with their own code, and
  there is no write route at all. `orchestration/workers.py` and the n8n examples are unchanged, no message is
  sent, and 26 new tests (55 in the phase file, **457 in the whole suite**) cover the stage projection, the five
  send states, the guard, the transaction boundary, redaction of secrets, demo/approval references, reply
  threading, the CLI, the API's read-only surface, and a report that survives a record pointing at something deleted.

### Added — 2026-10-15 (Phase 46: persistent prospect engagement and customer-outcome ledger)

- `tracking/engagement.py` adds `EngagementLedger`, the persistent record of the steps after an outreach draft
  is approved: `engagements` (append-only event log), `proposals`, `follow_ups`, `outcomes` and `contact_blocks`
  in the existing `TrackingStore` state file, with the existing id and bounded-text guards and no new database
  or dependency. `tracking/store.py` declares the five collections in `_empty_state()`, and
  `tracking/database.py` persists them through the same `system_kv` document mechanism that `worker_routing`
  already uses (`KV_PERSISTED_COLLECTIONS`), so a Postgres or SQLite backend round-trips them without new DDL.
- Twelve `python -m tracking` commands: `engagement-record`, `response-record`, `outreach-mark-sent`,
  `follow-up-schedule`, `follow-up-update`, `due-follow-ups`, `proposal-create`, `proposal-update`,
  `outcome-record`, `opt-out`, `opt-out-revoke`, `ledger`. Refusals now print an `error_code` next to the
  message (e.g. `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`, `CONTACT_BLOCKED`, `OUTREACH_NOT_APPROVED`, `WON_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`) so a
  human can act on a rejection without guessing at it.
- The boundary between adjacent claims is now enforced rather than assumed. Marking a draft as sent requires an
  evidence note or URL and still stores `provider_verified: false` with
  `delivery_status: REPORTED_BY_OPERATOR`, so an unverified message can never read as a confirmed delivery; a
  DRAFT proposal cannot be marked presented, and `PRESENTED` needs evidence of the act; `WON` needs a
  customer-side evidence kind plus a contract, invoice, or payment reference and stores no revenue figure at
  all; an outcome may only move along declared transitions and repeating it returns `unchanged: true`; nothing
  derives rejection from silence, because `due-follow-ups` is read-only and `NO_RESPONSE` is only ever an
  operator's explicit observation about their own inbox.
- Follow-ups respect consent and volume: an opt-out cancels the open follow-ups it covers and blocks new
  follow-ups and further sends on that channel (`CONTACT_BLOCKED`), the per-lead per-channel send count is
  capped at `MAX_SENDS_PER_LEAD_PER_CHANNEL = 3` (only counting reported sends, not prepared drafts), a due
  date in the past is refused without `--allow-past-due`, and revoking an opt-out requires a stated reason and
  does not reschedule anything by itself. Email and phone shapes are redacted from every free-text field on
  write, records link to `lead_id`/`project_id`/`outreach_id` rather than copying contact data, and repeated
  commands are idempotent on a derived or supplied key so a retried event cannot duplicate.
- Tests (`tests/test_phase46_engagement_ledger.py`, 29) cover persistence and reload, a SQLite and a JSON
  round-trip, the truth rules above, the contact limit, cancellation after opt-out, the proposal and outcome
  lifecycles, illegal transitions, duplicate events, redaction, and the CLI. One test asserts by reading the
  source that the ledger imports no network, builder, deployer, or sender module and exposes no route. No
  message is sent by any part of this phase, no n8n workflow is activated, no live delivery or response API is
  integrated, and no `orchestration/api.py` endpoint was added, so nothing outside this machine can record its
  own success.

### Changed — 2026-10-15 (Phase 45: Research Got a Real Public-Information Path, and the Permission to Use It Stayed Out of the Request's Hands)

- **The seam the Phase 3 design left open is now filled, without touching the qualification logic.** `_evaluate_website` has always accepted a `website_observation` - `load`, `correct_business`, `poor_mobile`, `missing_contact`, `outdated_information`, and the rest - and nothing ever produced one except a hand-written fixture, which is why a real prospect's website could only ever be `UNKNOWN`. `research/live_source.py` is that producer: it emits the same vocabulary, so identity verification, fact reconciliation, evidence tiers, and the `QUALIFIED`/`REVIEW`/`SKIP` machine are reused unchanged instead of being duplicated next to a second opinion.
- **Only what the record already names.** The live source reads `originalWebsite` and - only if a host is explicitly allowlisted *and* `fetchPlatformProfiles` is true - a social profile URL from the same record. There is no search, no crawl, no sub-page discovery, no link following, so "wider research" cannot be smuggled in through a job input; the way to widen it is to change what an operator approved, in the settings file, on purpose. Platform hosts are refused by default precisely because their content sits behind a login: fetching them returns a page about the platform, not about the business.
- **Two keys, neither of which belongs to the caller.** Live research runs only with `AI_WEBSITE_AGENT_ALLOW_LIVE_RESEARCH=1` in the process environment *and* `research.allowLiveResearch: true` in `config/system.settings.local.json`; a CLI flag or `input.live_research` selects the source, it does not authorize it, and a refusal happens before any request object is built (`LIVE_RESEARCH_NOT_ENABLED`). The n8n pipeline intentionally does not pass the field at all: a workflow that grants itself permission to make outbound requests is the exact failure mode the deployment approval gate was built to prevent, and consistency here is worth more than a checkbox.
- **Every bound can be lowered and none removed.** `maxPagesPerCandidate` is clamped to 1-3, `maxRequestsPerRun` to 1-12, the timeout to 45s, the response cap to 2 MB, retries to 0-2 with `Retry-After` honoured once and bounded, and a minimum inter-request delay is applied. `robots.txt` is fetched *before* the page and counts against the same budget - when the rules cannot be read because the budget ran out, the conservative answer is to not fetch the page, recorded as `ROBOTS_DISALLOWED`/`robots_budget_exhausted` rather than assumed away.
- **An unexamined page never becomes a finding.** 401/403 → `ACCESS_RESTRICTED` with the note that no credential or bypass was attempted; 429 → `RATE_LIMITED`; a page that renders a login form → `LOGIN_WALL` whose recorded uncertainty says plainly that it is not evidence the business lacks a website; non-HTML → `NON_HTML_RESPONSE` and no interpretation; oversize, transport failure, and malformed markup are all recorded as what they are. In each of those cases *no* `website_observation` is emitted, so `_evaluate_website` answers `UNKNOWN` - the honest verdict for "we could not look" - and if nothing readable was obtained the source raises, which the pipeline turns into `LIVE_RESEARCH_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` and a failed job instead of a quiet empty success. `NO_WEBSITE` still requires absence in the reviewed candidate evidence, exactly as before.
- **Observations are countable properties, quoted with their timestamp.** The viewport `<meta>` tag, the count of distinct internal links and whether a `nav`/`menu` element exists, `tel:`/`mailto:`/contact-link presence, and the most recent year appearing in the page are what set flags - with `untrustworthy` and `broken_links` never asserted from a single document, and the copyright-year rule only able to say "the latest year in this page is 2019". Identity comes from the *page's* own title/`og:site_name` plus city/country text that actually occurs in it, never copied from the candidate, so corroboration means something; a page that does not name the business is recorded as limited evidence and left unassessed. Each finding carries `source_url`, `observed_at`, a bounded `excerpt`, and a `retrieval` block, and the existing evidence-tier logic files it as `PRESERVED_PUBLIC_OBSERVATION`.
- **Research stayed in its lane, and the tests say so in both directions.** A modern, verified page yields `WEBSITE_MODERN` and a `SKIP` - research concluding "do not pitch this one" - while a poor page yields `WEBSITE_NEEDS_IMPROVEMENT` and `QUALIFIED_FOR_ASSESSMENT` with `website_ownership` still `WEBSITE_OWNERSHIP_UNCERTAIN` and the scorer's own hedge intact. The permitted live `RESEARCH` job was asserted to create no project, build, deployment, outreach package, review, or approval, and its output carries `live_research` accounting - requests used against the budget, plus one entry per attempted URL including `NOT_FETCHED_PLATFORM_RESTRICTED` - so what was deliberately left alone is as visible as what was read.
- **Stated limit rather than implied coverage:** no real business page has been fetched from this sandbox, where outbound access to arbitrary domains cannot be verified; the fetch, robots, retry, and observation contracts are proven against an injected transport (`patch("research.live_source.urlopen")`), and the first real run is an operator action on a permitted machine. 28 new tests, focused runs (57 tests across research/n8n/orchestration/scout/phase 25/44) green, full suite **402/402**, `python3 -m compileall -q .` clean, `git diff --check` clean, tracked review artifact unchanged.

### Changed — 2026-10-15 (Phase 44: The Live Discovery Source Made Reachable, the Priority Made a Real Selection, and Consent Split Between the Person and the Machine)

Focused runs (`test_scout_agent`, `test_phase25_discovery`, `test_orchestration`, `test_n8n_integration`, `test_cloud_deployment_readiness`) green at 43 tests, then **374/374** for the full suite (18 new), `python3 -m compileall -q .` clean, `git diff --check` clean, tracked review artifact `preview-export/lead-0002-pitstop-lagos/index.html` unchanged at `6d58ba25…e99b`; `project-0003` remains `READY_FOR_REVIEW` with `review_status PENDING` and the gate still answering `APPROVAL_MISSING`.
